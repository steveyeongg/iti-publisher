ITI Publisher

ITI Publisher is a private tool used only by the creator of Inside the Invention, a short-video series that shows how engineering, technology and AI actually work.

It uploads the series’ own finished videos to the series’ own YouTube channel at scheduled times, and reads that channel’s analytics for a weekly performance review. It is not offered to other users and does not access anyone else’s account.
